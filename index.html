Our Croydon commitment

Six promises. One borough.

We've built our entire business around the Council's six Social Value themes. Not because we have to — because Croydon raised us, and we believe in giving back.

SV1

Jobs & Skills

Every new contract creates apprenticeships and full-time roles recruited exclusively through Croydon Works. We pay the Real Living Wage from day one.

SV2

Growth

Over 80% of our suppliers are Croydon SMEs. From the garage that services our vehicles to the printer for our paperwork — we buy local first.

SV3

Health & Wellbeing

Mental Health First Aid trained team. Free counselling for staff. We sponsor Croydon community wellbeing events year-round.

SV4

Equality

Our team mirrors our borough. We actively recruit from groups underrepresented in the waste industry, including people rebuilding their lives.

SV5

Stronger Communities

Free fly-tip clearance for community groups, schools and TRAs. We've cleared over 40 sites for free since launching.

SV6

Cleaner & Greener

94% of waste diverted from landfill today. Investing in electric tippers and HVO biofuel to hit net zero by 2030.
